---
title: "Using a Deep Borehole Array to Inform Earthquake Focal Depths"
published: true
categories:
  - Blog
---

We leverage dense station coverage and waveform data from a 2km downhole geophone array to create a dataset of independently depth-constrained shallow earthquakes. Compared to a conventional location algorithm, we use waveform moveout up and down the vertical array and analyst-picked P and S arrivals to minimize misfit throughout a 3D grid search. Our work illustrates systemic mislocation of shallow events and yields a high-confidence set of colocated shallow and deeper events, further study of which will allow us better understand source characteristics of shallow seismicity.
